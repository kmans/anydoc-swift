# 1.  Introduction

The east quay was built in three stages. The first stage, a timber jetty of twenty berths, dates from the start of the register; the second doubled it in stone, and the third, the long breakwater, shelters both from the south-west swell.

Every berth has a number, a length in metres and a holder. The register is kept on paper and in a table; the two are compared each spring, and the differences are explained in the notes of the harbour master. [^1]

*“A harbour is a promise that the sea will wait.”*

Visitors are asked to call the harbour office on channel 9 before they come in, to give the length of their boat and its draught, and to say how many nights they plan to stay.

# 3.  Lists

- Water at every berth

  - cold, from the quay
  - hot, in the shower block

- Electricity

  - 16 A at berths 1 to 40

    - with a meter
    - without a meter

  - 32 A at the east pontoon

- Wi-Fi on the quay

Numbered, with other numbering styles at each level:

1. Call the office on channel 9

2. Give the length and the draught

   - a) length over all, in metres
   - b) draught, in metres

3. Take the berth you are given

   - a) tie up bow first
   - b) tie up stern first

4. Pay at the office
